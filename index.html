hi there

I’m Annie Finnerty, a technical writer and user-focused software developer. I love translating complex ideas into clear results.

Great products bring together thoughtful engineering, clear communication, and attention to the people using them. I love learning new technologies, exploring unfamiliar problems, and asking questions that help me understand both the technical details and the bigger picture. My path from English and anthropology into software engineering, technical writing, and UX reflects my enthusiasm for learning and connecting different perspectives.

I live outside Denver, Colorado. When I'm not at the computer, you can find me hiking, reading, or experimenting with new recipes.
